== Description ==

Digimia is a block theme for selling digital downloads with Easy Digital Downloads: templates, design kits, fonts, software, and other files people buy and download.

It comes with a store home page, product pages with a purchase card and related products, a product catalog, a shop with a sidebar, and designs for the cart and checkout, purchase confirmation, receipt, sign in, create account, and customer account pages. About, pricing, contact, and blog pages complete the site. Every design is made of block patterns you can change in the Site Editor.

The look is teal and lime by default. Four style variations, five more color presets, and five font pairings are in the Site Editor's Styles panel. Every design has a dark mode, and the theme supports right-to-left languages, with Arabic fonts for each font pairing.

The store designs need the free Easy Digital Downloads plugin. Appearance → Digimia installs it for you.
